# Frequently asked questions

## Is this live operational data?

No. Names, assets, devices, tickets and submissions are fictional.

## Can I try the forms?

Yes. Use sample values. Changes appear in related demo screens and remain in the current browser tab.

## Does admin access change real accounts?

No. Admin controls edit fictional records only. Password reset shows a demo-only result and does not authenticate anyone.

## Why are the map and Android app not included?

Those original links lead to separate applications outside the STS folder.
