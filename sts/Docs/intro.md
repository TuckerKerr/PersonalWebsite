# Student IT Field Services

STS brings campus IT support into one interface: equipment returns, deliveries, e-waste tracking, room checks, printer health and team activity.

## Portfolio demo

All records are fictional. Explore the worker and admin dashboards without an account. Updates stay in this browser tab.

## Explore

Submit a field-work report, then open the admin dashboard to review it. Return equipment and find it in Raw Data. Check a printer and see its consumable levels.
